# Corrections from a review of the whole site

A review of every page, after Readmissions joined the family, found the errors below. Each is fixed.

## Statements about the programs

- The Readmissions program cuts a hospital’s payments when its risk-adjusted readmissions run above its peer group’s median, not “above expected”: since fiscal year 2019 CMS has compared each hospital with the median of its peer group. The front page and the program’s description said “above expected”.
- Readmissions’ 3% cap and value-based purchasing’s 2% withhold are shares of base payments, not of all Medicare inpatient pay, as the front page and the programs’ descriptions implied.
- The quality-reporting and electronic health records cuts are a quarter and three-quarters of the market-basket increase, not of the yearly payment increase a hospital receives.
- Maryland’s hospitals: CMS scores them in the hospital-acquired conditions program and uses their data in the Readmissions program’s calculations; none of the three programs adjusts their pay. The front page and their hospital pages said they were not in these programs.
- The note on who is not in the Readmissions program now names Puerto Rico’s hospitals.

## Numbers

- 67 Readmissions hospital pages printed one of CMS’s excess readmission ratios or payment weights one digit off in the last place (1.0051 for CMS’s 1.0050), from a difference in rounding. Each now prints as CMS published it.
- The hospital pages printed each hospital’s value-based purchasing factor to four places; CMS publishes it to as many as ten, and the dollars beside it were computed from all ten. The factor now prints in full.
- Some Readmissions pages printed a hospital’s share of dual-eligible patients 0.1 point low, from rounding.

## Sentences the numbers didn’t bear out

- The front page’s hospital-acquired conditions step called 147 hospitals’ cut their “only” one; 39 of them also take a value-based purchasing cut. It now says they have no Readmissions cut.
- Eight hospital-acquired conditions hospital pages named a measured result as the one that added the most to the score when a measure CMS assigned added more.
- Hospital pages for 38 hospitals showed a fiscal year 2026 Readmissions cut with no word on why the hospital has no Readmissions page; CMS’s fiscal year 2027 file doesn’t list them, and the page now says so.

## Earlier

- On 2026-11-02 the front page’s hospital-acquired conditions step was reworded without an entry here. It had said “The 710 hospitals in the quarter with the highest scores”, but one of the hospitals CMS cut had no score. It now counts the scored hospitals CMS cut and the one it cut without a score.
