# The footer's year, and one likely range for each dollar figure

## The footer's year

Every page's footer gave the family's fiscal year 2026 estimates under the heading "Data as of", including the Readmissions pages, which show CMS's fiscal year 2027 file. The heading now reads "Family estimates, FY2026", and the Readmissions program page and the Readmissions card on the front page say which year each one shows.

## Likely ranges

When one program makes a hospital's whole net, the net now takes that program's likely range, the one its own pages give those dollars, instead of the range for a net of all three. A hospital whose net is its hospital-acquired conditions cut alone had shown two ranges for the same dollars, such as −$112K to −$63K beside $64–109K. The estimates themselves didn't change.

Of the 506 hospitals with an estimate whose net comes from one program alone, the printed range changed on 335 hospital pages and in the dollar statement on 332 hospital-acquired conditions hospital pages. On the rest it rounds the same.
