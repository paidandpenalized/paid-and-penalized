# The dual-eligible share, from CMS's full value

Readmissions hospital pages give each hospital's share of dual-eligible stays to one decimal place, on the record card and in Worksheet R. They rounded CMS's four-decimal value, and a fix listed in the other entries dated November 9, 2026 rounded a share that those four decimals put exactly on a half up. CMS's Excel file gives the share to more decimals, and for 149 hospitals it sits just under the half. For example, CMS's four decimals give 010040's share as 0.1515, which the page printed as 15.2%; the full value is 0.151468, or 15.1%. The data now keeps the share to eight decimals, enough that every page prints it as CMS's full value rounds, and each of these 149 pages now prints it 0.1 point lower.
